Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? Start here.

What is a micro-credential program?

A micro-credential program is focused training built around a defined set of skills or competencies. These six programs cover specialized topics in AI, data handling, machine learning, automation, cybersecurity, and cyber threat intelligence.

Do I need a college degree?

The submitted learner profiles do not list a college degree as a mandatory requirement. Data Collection and Annotation Level II and Generative AI Level III explicitly require a high school diploma or equivalent. Other programs have different stated prerequisites, so review each one carefully.

Do I need programming experience?

Machine Learning Model Development and AI Prompting for Automation list a background or interest in mathematics, statistics, and basic programming as preferred, not mandatory. Their mandatory learner profile specifies computer and basic digital literacy. Prior programming knowledge can nevertheless be helpful.

Can beginners join these programs?

Applicants with basic computer and digital literacy may satisfy the stated knowledge requirements for several programs. Data Collection and Annotation and Generative AI also require a high school diploma or equivalent. Cyber Risk Assessment specifically requires foundational cybersecurity knowledge.

Is cybersecurity work experience mandatory?

No. The Cyber Risk Assessment learner profile requires knowledge of fundamental cybersecurity concepts and preferably at least one year of relevant job or industry experience. The experience is a preference rather than an explicit mandatory condition.

What is the learning delivery format?

The submitted proposals identify blended learning as the training design. Confirm the actual online and in-person arrangements with the participating training center.

Will I receive a credential after completing training?

The programs are presented as micro-credential training. Specific completion documentation, assessment arrangements, and credential issuance conditions should be confirmed with the participating training center before enrollment.

When can I enroll?

Contact your participating training center for current intake availability, confirmed class dates, and enrollment instructions. Training schedules can vary by center and batch.
